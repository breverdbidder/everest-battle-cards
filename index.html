195-checkpoint v1.2 protocol. Phase-by-phase Ariel review. ≥90% green benchmark. No advance without approval.

14-point enterprise gate polls 30s, 15min timeout, 3 outcome branches. No Telegram until verdict. WIP < 14 = 0.

5. Push to Pages
