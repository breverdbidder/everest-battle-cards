The asymmetry

$10.2M Dono outranks $75M Orbital as a threat. Proximity of corpus and buyer beats size of war chest: Dono is assembling county-records data forty minutes from our market with an investor-adjacent customer; Orbital is renting workflow to lawyers an ocean away from the auction. Threat scales with overlap, not headcount — parity: Dono n/a-HIGH on use case, Orbital 0.38.

The BidDeed + ZoneWise wedge

Only full hand

In the Dono–Orbital–Mapwise–PropertyOnion matrix, BidDeed+ZoneWise alone holds corpus + AI + the investor buyer simultaneously: 356K labeled auction outcomes, 10.5M parcels, Shapira models.
