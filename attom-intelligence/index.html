No ATTOM twin — the beat

§15 the bid

Entry bid · SIGNAL$ Max Bid already net of surviving liens · walk-away · BID / REVIEW / SKIP, behind the legal disclaimer. ATTOM prints what the house is; nothing on their pages tells a bidder what to pay.

§16–17 lien hierarchy

FORECLOSING / SENIOR / JUNIOR / RELEASED / SUPER_PRIORITY per instrument with the statute (§48.23 · §197.122 · §197.552 · §713.07 · §720.3085 · §718.116 · 26 USC §7425) and joinder vs the LP party list. UNRESOLVED is printed, never guessed. 25 live OR counties, avg parity 30.8%, 55 upcoming at parity (1:29 PM ET).

§3 distressed market

A county question returns clearing ratio + outcome counts (sold-to-assessed, sold-to-judgment, third-party share) from verified Florida outcomes — not a housing chart.

§ZW the pairing

ZoneWise: DOR land use, jurisdiction, district (or PENDING), just value, envelope where built — what the dirt can become, on the same page as the bid.

§18 outcome writeback

After the sale the report grades its own call: ceiling held / walked correctly / clearing multiple.

§ML Shapira Models — WITHHELD

Model version printed; probability withheld until out-of-time validation on verified outcomes passes. Sep 11: V4 prod AUC 0.478 FC / 0.391 TD; v5b candidate 0.63 / 0.885, not passed. Printing the honest gate is the feature.
