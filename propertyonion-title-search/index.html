Where we beat both

The only survival engine

PropertyOnion writes one analyst sentence; Dono has no investor logic at all. We classify every recorded instrument with the statute that governs it and the recording position that decides it, then feed the SIGNAL$ Max Bid. No competitor prices the lien stack into the bid.

Joinder, not just a list

A junior mortgage whose holder was not named in the lis pendens is not wiped (§48.23). The engine reads the LP party list and says UNRESOLVED with the citation — the exact defect that costs bidders the most.

$25 and instant, before the sale

Pre-harvested on a rolling 14–21-day window off our own auction calendar; the report is complete the moment a customer pays, not 1–2 business days later.

Honesty as a feature

Coverage, sources searched, effective date, sha256, and every UNRESOLVED reason print on the report. PO's fine print says "verify yourself"; we show what was and was not searched.
